=== Step 1: Functional Dependencies ===
1. StudentID -> StudentName, CourseName
2. CourseName -> FacultyName, DepartmentName

=== Step 2: Normalization Steps ===
1NF: Ensure all attributes are atomic and each record is unique.
2NF: Eliminate partial dependencies. Since StudentID is a single-attribute key, it is already in 2NF.
3NF: Eliminate transitive dependencies. FacultyName and DepartmentName transitively depend on StudentID through CourseName.

=== Step 3: Final 3NF Schema ===
Table 1 Name: Course (CourseName [PK], FacultyName, DepartmentName)
Table 2 Name: Student (StudentID [PK], StudentName, CourseName [FK])
